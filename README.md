# 🌸 Puella Magi Madoka Magica

## 🧙‍♀️ Overview
**Puella Magi Madoka Magica** (also known as *Mahou Shoujo Madoka★Magica*) is a **dark magical girl anime** created by Gen Urobuchi and produced by Shaft and Aniplex.  
It’s famous for its **emotional story**, **unique art style**, and **unexpected twists**.

---

## ✨ Main Characters
- **Madoka Kaname** – A kind and gentle girl who wants to help others.  
- **Homura Akemi** – A mysterious transfer student with a secret.  
- **Sayaka Miki** – Madoka’s best friend with a strong sense of justice.  
- **Mami Tomoe** – An experienced magical girl who mentors the others.  
- **Kyubey** – A strange creature that offers to grant wishes... for a price.

---

## 💭 Why I Love It
> “Even miracles have a price.”

I love *Madoka Magica* because it looks like a typical cute magical girl show,  
but it turns into a deep and emotional story about **hope, sacrifice, and destiny**.  
The animation and music are also breathtaking! 🎶
